# Data quality and acceptance boundaries

The local audit parsed 114 unique PDFs and found 3,396 pages. It verified 2,996 page OCR checkpoints, 7,587 article candidates, 2,794 passages, 10,381 chunks, 185 zones, 6,579 lots, 12,006 GIS candidates and 93,495 RDF triples. Article-to-PDF and chunk-to-article-page references passed, and all chunk text hashes matched. The 352 articles lacking an OCR checkpoint are native-text candidates. The 24 lot-to-zone misses, 13 invalid geometries and 2,037 missing geometries remain explicit quality flags. These figures are structural checks, not factual accuracy results.

Thirty OCR pilot pages still require independent reference transcription. Twelve empty OCR outcomes have alternatives but no automatic adjudication. The 106 document references include candidate, ambiguous and locally unavailable identities; no identity is confirmed solely by a zone name. The 6,905 review packets remain pending. Published prices can conflict with SQL-derived prices, and the 720 public plot observations are dated; absence from that list never implies sold or reserved. No legal text or plot availability is production validated.

The public-repository release is **NO-GO for source-data distribution and production retrieval** until source redistribution permission, privacy review and human validation are documented. It is **GO for development of schemas, local verification and synthetic multilingual evaluation**. The protected local candidate copy is structurally usable by authorized reviewers but must not be sent to external AI services or GitHub.

The historical archive audit reported 2,852 AFI capture bodies, but `AFI_Replica_Local.zip` was not found in the current user workspace during this consolidation. The local raw web directory contains PDF copies, and processed web candidates retain source references; the original HTML/JSON capture bodies therefore cannot be independently re-exported from the currently available files. This is a preservation/recovery gap, not evidence that those captures never existed.

The local heuristic privacy screen checked 114 PDFs and 25,080 OCR/knowledge/web candidate records. It found email-shaped text in 5 PDFs, 39 OCR pages, 1 article, 58 institutional passages, 59 chunks and 16 institutional page candidates; the SQL dump contained 141 email-shaped occurrences. No credential-shaped strings were detected by this screen. These indicators can include public institutional contacts, and scanned personal data may be missed; no record was automatically cleared or removed.
