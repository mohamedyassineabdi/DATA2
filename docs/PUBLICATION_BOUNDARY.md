# Public distribution decision

The project owner authorizes publication of eligible Dalila work to this public repository. The owner also confirmed that there is **no written redistribution permission** for the AFI-supplied SQL, PDFs, captured institutional content or their text derivatives. Public accessibility and hackathon supply do not themselves establish a license to republish an entire corpus. Historical `restricted` labels were not treated as conclusive: the local audit inspected dataset families and source fields, and prepared a field-level public plot observation candidate. That candidate is still held locally because its source reuse basis is unresolved. The [APII official legal notice](https://www.tunisieindustrie.nat.tn/fr/doc.asp?mcat=25&mrub=205) explicitly requires prior written authorization for reproduction, download and distribution of site content outside its stated private, noncommercial-copy exception. No comparable reuse grant has been documented for the other captured institutional sources; their status remains source-specific review rather than a universal prohibition.

The raw SQL dump has 13 tables and 15,841 rows, including historical chatbot tables and lot fields for promoters and contracts. The normalized 6,579-lot candidate contains a narrower field set, but field removal alone does not resolve source rights. The 720-row captured plot list contains public-facing zone name, lot number, surface and price fields; it is a dated observation, not a live availability feed. Neither it nor the 114 PDFs, 2,996 OCR pages, 7,587 article fragments, 2,794 passages, 10,381 chunks, GIS records or RDF instance graph is pushed publicly at this stage.

To clear a family for public release, record a source-specific license or written AFI/institutional permission that covers public redistribution of the original material and derivatives, then run record-level privacy and credential screening. SQL requires a table/column/row projection and a separate review of promoter, contract and chatbot records. PDF and web content require source-by-source license and personal-data checks. Preserve source hashes, capture dates and candidate status in any released derivative. A restricted teammate transfer requires an identified recipient, access approval and encryption; the public repository is not that transfer channel.

The local audit artifacts and protected candidate copy live at `local_only/` in DATA2 and are ignored by Git. The original source and frozen release stay in the old workspace. Nothing in the local review folder is approved for external AI transmission.
